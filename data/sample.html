Acme Employee Handbook

Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried days expire on March 31.

Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted through the employee portal.

Equipment reimbursement

Employees can claim up to $500 per calendar year for approved home office equipment. Submit receipts within 30 days of purchase through the finance portal.
